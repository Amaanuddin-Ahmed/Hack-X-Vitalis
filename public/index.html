Food Lens · optional

A label is more useful with context.

Food Lens reads the nutrition details visible on a package, then brings the extracted facts together with profile preferences and risk estimates.

  • ✓ Nutrition facts, ingredients, and visible allergens
  • ✓ Highlights and considerations in plain language
  • ✓ Suitability guidance based on your stated context

Image extraction can miss or misread details. Check the original packaging, especially for allergens.

Care with personal information

Private by default, clear by design.

The current prototype keeps structured session results in your browser. Image files are sent only when you choose to analyze them, and are not stored in that session record.

Read the safeguards ↓

A responsible prototype

Useful context, honest limits.

Vitalis is an educational demonstration, not a medical device. Model outputs are estimates and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for an individual. They are not diagnoses or treatment recommendations.

Chest X-ray observations use research software and must not guide clinical decisions. Remove identifying information before choosing to upload any medical image. For health concerns, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
